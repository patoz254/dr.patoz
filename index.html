About Me

Compassionate care
rooted in science

I'm Dr. Amara Osei, a licensed clinical psychologist with over 12 years of experience helping individuals navigate life's most challenging moments. My approach blends cognitive-behavioral therapy, mindfulness, and somatic techniques to create lasting, meaningful change.


I believe therapy should feel like a genuine human conversation — warm, honest, and free of judgment. My practice is a safe, affirming space for people of all backgrounds.
